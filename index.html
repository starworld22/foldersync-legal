Folder Sync

Back up a folder from your Android phone to your own Google Drive, on a schedule.

What it does

  • You choose a folder on your phone; the app uploads its files to your own Google Drive.
  • You choose when and how often it runs. It works quietly in the background.
  • A companion app, Co FolderSync, lets a second phone you own (for example a parent's phone) see what has been backed up and change the sync settings, after the two phones are linked with a one-time code.
