Privacy Policy

Folder Sync & Co FolderSync · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Folder Sync and its companion app Co FolderSync ("the apps") are provided by the app's developer. This policy explains what the apps access and why. The apps are designed so your files stay in your own accounts — the developer does not receive or store your files.

What the apps access

  • A folder you choose on your phone. Folder Sync reads the files in the folder you select so it can upload them. It uses Android's folder picker and only gets access to the folder you pick.
  • Your Google Drive (drive.file scope). When you connect Google Drive, the app can create and manage only the files it creates in your Drive. It cannot see or touch your other Drive files. Your files are uploaded to your own Google Drive.
  • Linking information (Firebase). To link the two phones, the apps use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The apps store: a random pairing code, a random anonymous sign-in identifier for each phone, your chosen sync settings, the time and result of the last sync, and a list of the names, sizes, dates and Drive identifiers of files that were backed up. This lets the linked phone show what was backed up and change the settings. The list contains file information, not the file contents.

What the apps do NOT do

  • They do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • They do not record phone calls, read your messages, or collect your contacts.
  • They do not send your files to the developer or to any server other than your own Google Drive.
  • They show no ads and contain no advertising trackers.

Linking two phones

Co FolderSync can monitor and control a phone running Folder Sync only after someone enters, on the controlling phone, the one-time code shown on the other phone. Both phones are intended to belong to the same person or family (for example, a parent monitoring a child's phone with the child's awareness). You can unlink at any time from within the app.

Data retention

Sync settings and the backed-up-file list remain in Firebase until you unlink or uninstall. Files uploaded to your Google Drive remain in your Drive until you delete them there. Deleting a backed-up file in Google Drive removes it from the phone on the next sync, by design.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Drive, choose a different folder, or turn off automatic sync at any time.
  • Revoke the app's Google Drive access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstall the apps to stop all access.
